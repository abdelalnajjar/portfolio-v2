03 / Research direction

From video to a privacy-aware alert.

My current research explores how a fall-detection system can understand movement without depending on a person’s identity, clothing, or raw appearance.

The architecture separates perception, temporal reasoning, privacy rendering, and human-readable alerts into clear modules. That makes the system easier to test, improve, and explain.
